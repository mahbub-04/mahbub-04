## About

I work on statistical methods for population health, mostly with survey and clinical data from Bangladesh. Since September 2025 I have been a research assistant in the Department of Statistics at MBSTU, where I handle the analysis for the group's health studies, from data cleaning to model checking. I also work part-time as a data scientist on credit-risk data.

## Research interests

- Survival and competing-risks models for complex survey data
- Spatial models of health outcomes and their social and environmental drivers
- Interpretable and well-calibrated machine learning for health prediction, including radiomics
- Reproductive, child and population health, and cancer outcomes
